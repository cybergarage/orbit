# Orbit logo and name

The selected logo is the bold E concept: an orbital O, a central dot, and a
satellite dot, accompanied by the Orbit wordmark. The selected color variants
are 01 (navy and blue) and 05 (monochrome).

## Artwork

- [Original color-study template](orbit-e-color-patterns.png): 1774 × 887 PNG,
  preserved without changes. The sheet also shows other color studies.
- [01: navy and blue](../img/orbit-logo-navy-blue.png): 2172 × 724 PNG with
  transparency.
- [05: monochrome](../img/orbit-logo-monochrome.png): 2172 × 724 PNG with
  transparency.
- [README artwork](../img/orbit-logo-navy-blue-readme.png): 2172 × 724 opaque
  PNG with a light background, used to keep the dark logo legible on both
  light and dark README backgrounds.

These are generated raster PNG images. No vector master was supplied.
The transparent variants contain dark artwork and need a light background
for legible display.

## Meaning of the name

Orbit means “orbit.” Just as a satellite fulfills its role along an orbit,
agents move work forward in line with people's goals. The name expresses the
idea of creating that path.

### Original Japanese wording

The following is the user-provided source text for the English explanation:

「Orbitは『軌道』を意味します。人工衛星が軌道に沿って役割を果たすように、エージェントが人の目的に沿って仕事を進める。その道筋をつくる、という意味を込めています。」
